SMS Privacy Notice

This notice explains how BinRun handles mobile phone numbers and information used for optional service-related text messages.

Information used

When a customer opts in, BinRun uses the customer's name, mobile number, service schedule, service status, message history, and (when selected) a service-proof photo to prepare and send requested service notifications.

How information is used

Mobile numbers and SMS consent information are used to send operational updates about BinRun service, such as bins being placed out or returned. They are not used by BinRun for unrelated marketing messages.

Sharing

BinRun does not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or consent records with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. The messaging provider processes the phone number and message content only to transmit and support the requested messages. Service photos are stored in a private cloud storage bucket and are shared through a signed link only when the customer update includes a photo.

Photo links and retention

Photo proof links expire after 30 days. Anyone who receives a signed link may be able to view that photo while the link is active. BinRun retains the service record and photo in its private business storage until the owner removes it or changes the storage policy.

Customer choices

Text updates are optional and are not required to buy BinRun service. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Customers may also contact BinRun using the contact information provided when arranging service to ask questions about their contact information.

Security

BinRun uses authenticated cloud services, access controls, and private photo storage. No website or online storage system can guarantee absolute security. Customers should not forward private photo links to people who should not see the photo.
