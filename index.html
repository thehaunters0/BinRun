DOOR-TO-DOOR HELPER

Sales & pricing

Quick scripts and a calculator to use while talking with neighbors.

Your introduction

“Hi! My name is Elias, and I live nearby. I'm offering a neighborhood trash and recycling bin service. I'll take your bins out before pickup and bring them back afterward, so you don't have to worry about it. It's $5 for one week, with discounts if you want multiple weeks. Would you be interested?”

If they say yes

“Awesome, thank you! I also offer a discount if you'd like multiple weeks. The first week is $5, and each additional week is just $4.”

Then ask which day their garbage is collected. Explain that you'll take the bins out around 7 PM the evening before and check during the next day's return sweeps until the truck has collected them. Ask how many weeks they'd like and record their details in Customers.

If they say no

“No problem at all! Thanks for your time. Have a great day!”

If they need to think about it

“Of course! No pressure. Thanks for listening, and have a great day!”

If they ask what happens if the truck hasn't collected their bins

“I check during the day and come back later if the truck hasn't picked them up yet. I keep checking until they've been collected, then I bring the bins back.”

BINRUN FIELD GUIDE

How to run your business

Use BinRun in this order

1
Today

See the selected collection week and the next service action. Start the correct route from the run sheet.

2
Route

Optimize the stops, follow the route, and open each stop's completion screen when you arrive.

3
Photo & text

Optionally take a photo, review or edit the customer text, then tap Send. The service is recorded when the provider accepts the text.

4
Customers

Keep each household's address, pickup weekday, plan, phone number, notes, and text permission in one place.

5
Payments

Record Cash or Apple Cash after you actually receive it. BinRun updates the outstanding balance.

Most days you only need Today and Route. Customers is your address book; Schedule and service records are occasional tools under More.

Customers — your permanent household list

  1. Tap Add and enter the customer's name, full address, and contact details.
  2. Set the municipal garbage collection weekday and the first collection date. The first collection date must be the actual date the truck is expected.
  3. Choose a service plan. Full service is both bins out and back. It costs $5 for the first week and $4 for each additional week. Use the flexible package option for a multi-week plan. Partial service is generally $3 per week; custom combinations should use the agreed total.
  4. Check the task boxes carefully. They define what BinRun should do for every week (or each custom week).
  5. Choose when payment is due, enter the agreed price, and add placement/access notes. Record terms acceptance only after the customer actually agrees.
  6. Save. Do not add them again next week—the subscription creates future jobs for the same customer.

Paused customers stop generating future unfinished jobs. Cancelled customers are no longer active. Edit them if they restart.

Schedule — the calendar of work to do

A customer is the household. A job is one service week for that household. One customer on a 4-week package normally has four separate scheduled jobs.

  • The date shown is collection day (when the city truck is expected).
  • Take-out is planned for 7:00 PM the day before.
  • Return checks happen on collection day at 10:00 AM, 11:30 AM, and 1:00 PM.
  • Tap Use this week in Route for the collection week you are about to service. This tells Route which weekly jobs to show.

If no work appears, check that the customer is active and has a subscription, collection day, and first collection date. Use Refresh after editing customers.

Route — where you perform the service

Night before collection: choose Take bins out, review all selected customers, optimize the order, and follow the route. Mark the required take-out checklist as complete at each customer.

Collection day: choose Bring bins back and start with Sweep 1 at 10:00 AM. Visit every customer whose bins have not been returned, even if you suspect the truck has not arrived. This first visit is how you check.

  • If the truck collected the garbage, tap Collected — return bins and mark the return complete.
  • If the truck has not collected it, tap Still not collected. The customer stays pending.
  • At Sweep 2 (11:30 AM), visit everyone still pending. Repeat at Sweep 3 (1:00 PM) only for those still pending.
  • Never assign a customer to a sweep in advance. The sweep is simply the next time you check all bins that have not yet been returned.

When no customers remain pending, you are finished. You can skip later sweeps. The route order is a suggested travel order, not an automatic service completion.

Ops — your service notebook and exceptions desk

  • Daily run sheet: a quick count of take-outs, returns, and completed jobs for the selected week.
  • Service activity: add notes, customer contacts, photos, and evidence. Route checklist changes are also logged automatically.
  • Issues: record things such as a truck not collecting, damaged/missing bins, blocked access, bad weather, or a requested change. Mark the issue resolved only after you have handled it.
  • Customer updates: the Route completion screen prepares the text. Add a photo if you want, review it, tap Open Messages, then press Send in Messages. You do not need to open Ops.
  • Holiday adjustments: check the municipality's official notice, then move the affected service date. BinRun does not automatically know city holiday schedules.
  • Business terms: keep your standard service terms and contact details in one place.
  • Discounts/refunds: use only when changing a customer's invoice or recording money actually returned.

Payments — invoices are not the same as money received

  • An invoice is the bill: what the customer owes for a package or weekly service.
  • A payment is money you physically received. Record the date, amount, and whether it was Cash or Apple Cash.
  • Partial payments are okay. The remaining balance is calculated automatically.
  • A refund is a separate record for money you gave back. A discount changes the invoice amount owed; it is not a payment.
  • Check the customer's history for their invoices, service weeks, payments, refunds, and balance.

BinRun does not transfer money, charge cards, or confirm Apple Cash automatically. Only mark money received after you verify it in person or in Wallet.

Customer text updates and photo proof

  1. At a stop, tap Done · photo & text.
  2. Take a picture or choose one from your phone. BinRun uploads it to your private Supabase photo storage and creates a 30-day viewing link.
  3. Review the message. The photo link is added automatically; you can edit the wording.
  4. Tap Open Messages & complete stop. Your phone opens Messages with the customer number and draft filled in. You press Send.
  5. Return to BinRun when finished. Service is already saved. In the Service records list, you can optionally tap Mark text sent so your records reflect that you sent it.
  6. If you do not want to text, choose Complete service without text.

BinRun cannot detect whether Messages actually sent the draft. It records that Messages was opened, not that the customer received a text. Only text customers who have agreed to receive service updates. Anyone who receives the 30-day photo link can view that photo until it expires.

Your existing database

Vercel hosts your BinRun website. Supabase stores your private business data and handles your owner sign-in. Your browser also keeps a local working copy. Keep using your existing project and owner account.

For photo proof, BinRun uses a private Supabase Storage bucket. It does not replace your customer, job, invoice, or payment records.

  1. Back up BinRun from Settings → Full JSON backup.
  2. In your existing Supabase project, open SQL Editor → New query. Run the 20261124_binrun_manual_photo_proof.sql migration from this upgrade once. If you already ran the earlier v13 SMS/photo migration successfully, the photo bucket and policies already exist, so you can skip this SQL step.
  3. Keep your real backend-config.js unchanged in GitHub. Never put a service-role/secret key in the website.
  4. Deploy the new website files to the same GitHub repository and Vercel project.
  5. Your PC and iPhone continue to sync through the same owner account. Export a JSON backup regularly.

Customers do not need accounts. BinRun opens your phone's Messages app with a prepared text; you press Send. No Twilio account, paid messaging service, email SMTP, or custom domain is required.

Photo privacy: photos are stored in a private bucket. The signed link works for 30 days; anyone who receives it can view the photo until it expires. Avoid faces, house interiors, license plates, or sensitive material.

Your normal weekly routine

  1. Before a new customer starts: create their profile and check the weekday, first date, package, tasks, price, payment timing, and access instructions.
  2. Before service: review Schedule, confirm the city collection date, and select that week.
  3. At 7 PM the prior evening: open Route → Take bins out, build the route, complete every required take-out.
  4. On collection day: Route → Bring bins back → Sweep 1. Visit all still-pending customers and either return their bins or mark not collected.
  5. At 11:30 AM and 1 PM: repeat for remaining pending customers only. Stop early when none remain.
  6. After each stop: optionally add a photo, review the message, open Messages, and press Send. Or complete the service without texting.
  7. When payment is received: open Payments and record the amount and method. Follow up on any open balance.
  8. After a problem: open More → Service records & issues and leave it open until resolved.
  9. Occasionally: check the Schedule for date changes and export a backup.

What BinRun does not do yet

It does not determine whether the city truck has collected the garbage, transfer money, or automatically know municipal holiday schedules. Text messages are prepared in BinRun; you review and press Send in Messages and message and tap Send. Texts are prepared in your phone's Messages app; you press Send yourself. No paid texting provider is needed. Photo links are private, time-limited links; anyone who has the link can view the photo until it expires.
