BinRun

Text Message Terms

BinRun sends optional service-related text messages to customers who have explicitly agreed to receive them. Messages are operational updates about the customer's trash and recycling bin service, not marketing promotions.

Message examples: a notice that bins were placed out for collection, a notice that bins were returned, or a private link to an optional service-proof photo.

How to opt in

Customers may provide their mobile number and explicitly agree to service text updates verbally or in writing when arranging BinRun service. BinRun records the customer's permission and the method of consent in its customer record. Providing a phone number by itself does not mean the customer has agreed to receive texts. Text consent is optional and is not a condition of purchasing the bin service.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with the customer's service schedule and is typically up to two service updates per collection day (one for take-out and one for return). Additional messages may be sent only when needed for a service-related issue or reply. Message and data rates may apply according to the customer's wireless plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP to opt out of future text messages. Reply HELP for help. After opting out, service updates will no longer be sent by text unless the customer explicitly opts in again.

Photo proof

When BinRun includes a service photo, the text contains a private, time-limited viewing link. The link expires after 30 days. Anyone who receives the link may be able to view the photo while the link remains active, so customers should not share it publicly.

Contact

For questions about service notifications, contact BinRun using the contact information provided when arranging service.
